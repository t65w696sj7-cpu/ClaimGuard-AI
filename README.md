---

## Executive Summary

ClaimGuard AI is an independent enterprise solutions architecture case study designed around a real-world insurance challenge: modernizing claims processing with secure, scalable, and responsible artificial intelligence.

The project simulates an engagement with a mid-sized insurance carrier experiencing slow claims processing, manual document review, fragmented information, rising operational costs, and concerns surrounding the responsible adoption of generative AI.

The objective is not simply to build an AI application. The objective is to design, implement, test, and document an enterprise cloud solution while evaluating the business and technical tradeoffs involved in bringing AI into a regulated insurance environment.

---

## Business Problem

A fictional mid-sized insurance carrier processes thousands of claims containing accident reports, photographs, customer statements, policy information, estimates, and other supporting documents.

Claims adjusters currently spend significant time manually reviewing this information before determining the appropriate next action.

The organization wants to modernize this workflow while addressing several requirements:

- Reduce manual document review
- Improve claims processing efficiency
- Secure sensitive customer and claims information
- Provide AI-generated summaries grounded in claim documentation
- Identify inconsistencies for human review without allowing AI to make unsupported fraud determinations
- Maintain human oversight for consequential decisions
- Scale with changing claims volume
- Monitor system health and failures
- Control cloud and AI operating costs
- Design for backup, recovery, and service disruptions

---

## Proposed Solution

ClaimGuard AI will provide a cloud-based claims intelligence platform capable of:

1. Securely ingesting claim documents and supporting evidence.
2. Extracting and organizing relevant claim information.
3. Generating AI-assisted claim summaries.
4. Identifying potential inconsistencies requiring adjuster review.
5. Providing document-grounded question answering through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
6. Maintaining human review before consequential claims decisions.
7. Monitoring application health, security events, and operational failures.
8. Providing architecture designed around scalability, reliability, security, and cost control.
